Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about tickets, workshops, and the event.

What is included in each ticket type?

Standard passes grant full access to all keynotes and 48 technical talks across all 3 days. Pro and Team passes also unlock unlimited hands-on workshops, VIP networking dinner access, lifetime recordings, and 1-on-1 speaker mentorship.

Can I get a refund or transfer my ticket?

Yes. Full refunds are available up to 30 days prior to the conference (July 15, 2026). If you are unable to attend after that date, you can transfer your ticket to a teammate or colleague at any time free of charge.

Are meals and refreshments provided?

All attendees receive complimentary catered networking lunch, artisan coffee, and refreshments throughout all three days. Pro and Team ticket holders also gain entry to the exclusive evening networking dinner.

Do I need to reserve workshop seats in advance?

Workshops have limited seating to maintain high interactive quality. Pro and Team ticket holders will receive an email 3 weeks before the event with early access to choose and reserve their preferred workshop tracks.

Will session recordings be available after the conference?

Yes! Standard ticket holders receive 30 days of digital access to all recorded sessions. Pro and Team attendees receive lifetime access to high-definition video recordings and speaker presentation slides.

Are there discounts for students or non-profits?

We offer a 50% discount for accredited students and special pricing for registered non-profit teams. Please reach out to tickets@devconf2026.com with your credentials to apply.
